Software engineer building AI-native systems

I am a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at BUET, and I build systems where a language model has to be right rather than merely fluent. That work spans agentic architectures, applied machine learning, and the full-stack products around them.

The principle I keep arriving at from different directions is this: give the model the smallest job that only it can do, and make everything around it deterministic, measurable and reproducible. It is why my trading agent re-derives every published number from its own journal, and why my forensics layer refuses to assert anything it cannot ground in evidence.
